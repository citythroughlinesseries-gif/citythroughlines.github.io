THE CITY IS YOUR CANVAS.

From Colosseum to Vatican City, this edition explores the sights, local culture and visual character of Rome through a gallery of line illustrations.

Designed for adults who love travel, architecture and creative downtime, the pages use bold composition and open spaces for mindful colouring.
