INSIDE THE BOOK

THE CITY IS YOUR CANVAS.

From Canal House Facade to Anne Frank House, this edition explores the sights, local culture and visual character of Amsterdam through a gallery of line illustrations.

Designed for adults who love travel, architecture and creative downtime, the pages use bold composition and open spaces for mindful colouring.
