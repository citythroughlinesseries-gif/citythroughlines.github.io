A GALLERY OF LINES

17 NEW YORK ICONS.
17 STORIES TO COLOUR.

Discover the places and details that inspired the illustrations in this edition.

01

Statue of Liberty

Dedicated in 1886, the Statue of Liberty is one of New York’s most famous symbols, known for welcoming arrivals and representing freedom.

02

Brooklyn Bridge

Opened in 1883, the Brooklyn Bridge is famous for its historic suspension design and sweeping views over the East River.

03

Central Park

Opened in 1858, Central Park is famous as New York’s green heart, with lakes, paths, lawns, and a dramatic city backdrop.

04

Times Square

Developed in the early 20th century, Times Square is famous for its bright lights, theaters, and nonstop energy.

05

New York Public Library

Opened in 1911, the New York Public Library is famous for its grand Beaux-Arts architecture and iconic lion statues.

06

Empire State Building

Completed in 1931, the Empire State Building is famous for its Art Deco style and long-standing role in the New York skyline.

07

Yankee Stadium

Opened in 2009, Yankee Stadium is famous as the home of the New York Yankees and one of baseball’s most iconic venues.

08

Guggenheim Museum

Opened in 1959, the Guggenheim is famous for its spiraling modern design and exceptional art collection.

09

Chrysler Building

Completed in 1930, the Chrysler Building is famous for its Art Deco crown and elegant stainless-steel detailing.

10

Grand Central Terminal

Opened in 1913, Grand Central Terminal is famous for its vast concourse, celestial ceiling, and landmark status.

11

Yellow Cab

Standardized by city regulation in 1967 to easily identify official medallion taxis, the yellow cab is famous as an enduring pop-culture symbol of New York's busy street life.

12

Fifth Avenue

Developed in the 19th century, Fifth Avenue is famous for luxury shopping, major landmarks, and its central place in Manhattan.

13

Broadway

Established as New York’s theater district in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, Broadway is famous for its world-class stage productions.

14

H&H Bagels

First opened on the Upper West Side in 1972, H&H Bagels is famous for crafting the definitive kettle-boiled New York water bagel that became a citywide institution.

15

One World Trade Center

Completed in 2014, One World Trade Center is famous for its symbolic role in Lower Manhattan and its striking modern skyline presence.

16

Coney Island

Developed as a seaside amusement destination in the late 19th century, Coney Island is famous for its beach, boardwalk, and classic rides.

17

Flatiron Building

Completed in 1902, the Flatiron Building is famous for its triangular shape and early skyscraper design.
